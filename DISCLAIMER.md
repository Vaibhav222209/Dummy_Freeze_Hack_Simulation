## 1. Non-Affiliation & Fictional Nature Statement

I, **Vaibhav Thakur**, explicitly state that **I have no relation, affiliation, knowledge, or connection whatsoever with any real-world cyber incidents, threat actors, or events that have occurred in real life**.

**Mission La Surprise** was built solely as an **educational, academic cybersecurity awareness and UI/UX threat-modeling sample project** inspired broadly by public discussions on protecting critical food and healthcare infrastructure.

- **No Real Entities Targeted:** All grocery stores (*Marché Frais Bastille*, *Mercato Alimentare Brera*, *FrischeMarkt Mitte*, *Thames Fresh Grocers*) and hospital cryo-storage facilities (*Centre Médical Saint-Lazare*, *Ospedale San Lorenzo*, *Klinikum Spree-Nord*, *St. Jude's Bio-Cryo Wing*) depicted in Paris, Milan, Berlin, and London are **100% fictional**.
- **No Real Credentials or Personal Data:** All shopkeeper names, `.sandbox.invalid` email addresses, `+XX-XX-0000-XXXX` phone numbers, BMS PIN codes, and password hashes are **synthetic strings generated strictly for visual simulation**.
- **No Real Payment Card Data:** Every card record in the sample vault exclusively uses officially designated **non-routable PCI test prefixes** (`4000 0000 0000 XXXX` and `5555 5500 0000 XXXX`) with `000` test verification codes. None of the records correspond to any real person or financial institution.
- **No Exploit Code:** All Modbus/TCP, BACnet/IP, OPC-UA, and MQTT log lines displayed in the terminal are **harmless client-side animations** running inside React state.
